Privacy Policy

Shape Stories: Build & Learn

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Shape Stories: Build & Learn ("Shape Stories", "the app", "we", "our", or "us") is a preschool educational game developed by Sajid Ali.

Shape Stories is designed to provide young children with a simple, safe, enjoyable, and educational experience focused on recognizing shapes, understanding spatial relationships, matching objects, and building familiar pictures from basic geometric forms.

We take children's privacy seriously and have designed Shape Stories to minimize data collection and avoid unnecessary online services.

Our privacy-first approach: Shape Stories does not require an account, registration, login, child profile, email address, phone number, or custom online account. The app does not contain advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Normal game progress is stored locally on the device.

2. Children's Privacy

Shape Stories is designed primarily for preschool children, approximately ages 3 to 6. We therefore take additional care to minimize data collection and avoid unnecessary features that could create privacy risks for young children.

Shape Stories does not ask children to provide personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Precise location
  • Date of birth
  • School information
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Voice recordings
Shape Stories does not include child chat, messaging, public profiles, public leaderboards, user-generated content, social networking, or multiplayer communication.

3. Information Stored by the App

Shape Stories may store limited game and learning-progress information locally on the device so that the child can continue from where they previously stopped.

Local information may include:

  • Completed learning activities
  • Unlocked worlds or Story Cards
  • Learning progress
  • Shapes practiced
  • Local help or scaffolding usage
  • Local play-progress information
  • Selected accessibility settings
  • Sound, voice, haptic, and display preferences

This information is intended to remain on the user's device. Shape Stories does not operate a custom backend or cloud account system for storing child learning profiles.

4. Accounts and Registration

Shape Stories does not require children, parents, or other users to create a Shape Stories account.

The app does not require:

  • Registration
  • Login credentials
  • Email registration
  • Google sign-in inside the game
  • Facebook or social sign-in
  • Cloud profiles
  • Child usernames

A Google account may be used by Google Play itself for downloading, trial access where available, purchasing, licensing, security, and managing the app. Shape Stories does not create or receive a separate child account from that process.

5. Advertising

Shape Stories does not contain third-party advertising.

No advertising: Shape Stories does not use Google AdMob or another advertising network to show banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded ads, app-open ads, native ads, or personalized advertisements.

Children are not required to watch advertisements to complete activities, retry activities, unlock progress, receive learning help, or continue playing.

6. Paid App and Google Play Purchases

Shape Stories is intended to be distributed as a paid application through Google Play.

The purchase of Shape Stories is handled by Google Play outside the game's own payment systems. Shape Stories does not directly collect or process credit card numbers, bank account details, or other payment credentials.

Google Play may process information necessary to complete and manage purchases, verify licenses, prevent fraud, provide refunds where applicable, and maintain transaction records.

Payment information processed by Google is governed by Google's own terms and privacy practices.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy here:

Google Privacy Policy

7. Google Play Trial Access

Google Play may make a free trial or other trial experience available for eligible paid games in supported countries, accounts, or devices.

When such a trial is available, Google Play manages the trial, eligibility, access period, and purchase process.

Shape Stories does not operate its own child-facing trial payment system and does not require the child to enter payment information.

Trial availability and conditions are determined by Google Play and may vary by user, country, device, or Google Play policy.

8. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Shape Stories does not contain in-app purchases or subscriptions.

There are no child-facing purchases for coins, lives, boosters, levels, virtual items, additional content, or recurring memberships.

The intended business model is a one-time purchase of the complete application through Google Play.

9. Analytics and Tracking

Shape Stories does not use Firebase Analytics, advertising analytics, a custom analytics platform, or a custom server-based tracking system in the app.

Limited game-progress information may be calculated and stored locally on the device for gameplay and parent-facing learning-progress functionality.

Google Play may independently provide developers with aggregated information related to app distribution, purchases, installations, crashes, technical performance, security, or store performance as part of operating the Google Play platform.

Such Google Play platform processing is governed by Google's own policies.

10. Android Advertising Identifier

Because Shape Stories does not contain advertising, the app is not intended to use the Android Advertising ID for advertising, personalized advertising, remarketing, or advertising attribution.

The final production application's dependencies and merged Android manifest should be reviewed before release to confirm that unnecessary advertising-related permissions and SDKs are not included.

11. Location Information

Shape Stories does not request precise location permission.

We do not intentionally collect a child's precise GPS location.

12. Device Permissions

Shape Stories is designed to operate without requesting unnecessary sensitive device permissions.

The app does not intentionally require access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • SMS messages
  • Call logs
  • Precise location
  • Photos or videos
  • Nearby devices
  • Calendar
  • Health information

13. External Links and Parent Zone

Child-facing gameplay is designed not to expose ordinary external web links.

Links such as this Privacy Policy, developer information, licenses, legal information, or other external resources should be placed inside the Parent Zone and protected by an adult-oriented gate where appropriate.

14. Data Sharing

Shape Stories does not sell children's personal information.

We do not operate a commercial database of child profiles.

Because Shape Stories does not use advertising, a custom backend, or a custom analytics system, the app is designed to minimize transmission of child-related data to third parties.

Google may independently process limited information as part of operating Google Play, including app distribution, paid-app transactions, licensing, security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, refunds, and related platform functionality.

Any third-party library included in the final production build should be reviewed before release to ensure that its behavior is consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable children's privacy obligations.

15. Data Retention

Local game progress remains on the device until it is removed by the user, the app's Reset Progress function is used, the application is uninstalled, or Android clears the application's stored data.

Shape Stories does not maintain a custom cloud copy of the child's local game or learning progress.

Information processed independently by Google Play, including purchase and platform records, is subject to Google's own retention practices and policies.

16. Deleting Local Progress

Parents can delete the game's locally stored learning progress using the Reset Progress option inside the Parent Zone where that feature is available.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its application data through Android settings may also remove locally stored game information.

Because Shape Stories does not use a Shape Stories account or custom cloud profile, there is no remote Shape Stories account or child profile that needs to be deleted.

Deleting local progress does not necessarily delete Google Play purchase or transaction records maintained independently by Google.

17. Security

We use reasonable technical and product-design measures intended to minimize privacy risks.

These measures include limiting data collection, avoiding child accounts, avoiding advertising, avoiding unnecessary permissions, storing gameplay progress locally, restricting external links, and reviewing third-party dependencies before production release.

However, no software or electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

18. International Users

Shape Stories may be made available through Google Play in multiple countries and regions.

We aim to design the app consistently with applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play policies for child-directed applications.

Specific legal obligations may vary depending on the country or region where the app is offered.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, dependencies, data practices, payment model, legal requirements, or Google Play requirements change.

If Shape Stories later introduces any feature that materially changes its privacy practices, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or alongside that change where required.

When we update this policy, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

We encourage parents and caregivers to review this page periodically.
