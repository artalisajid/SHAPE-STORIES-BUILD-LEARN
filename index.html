Privacy Policy

Shape Stories: Build & Learn

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Shape Stories: Build & Learn ("Shape Stories", "the app", "we", "our", or "us") is a preschool educational game developed by Sajid Ali.

Shape Stories is designed to provide children with a simple, safe, and educational experience focused on learning and recognizing shapes, spatial relationships, and building familiar pictures from basic geometric forms.

We take children's privacy seriously and have designed the app to minimize the collection of personal information.

Our privacy-first approach: Shape Stories does not require an account, login, child profile, email address, phone number, or custom online account to play. Core game progress is stored locally on the device.

2. Children's Privacy

Shape Stories is designed primarily for preschool children. We therefore take additional precautions when designing the app, selecting third-party services, displaying advertising, and handling device information.

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Precise location
  • Date of birth
  • School information
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Voice recordings
Shape Stories does not include child chat, messaging, public profiles, public leaderboards, user-generated content, social networking, or multiplayer communication.

3. Information Stored by the App

Shape Stories stores limited game and learning-progress information locally on the device so the child can continue from where they stopped.

Local information may include:

  • Completed learning activities
  • Unlocked worlds or Story Cards
  • Learning progress
  • Shapes practiced
  • Aggregate help or scaffolding usage
  • Local play-time information
  • Selected accessibility settings
  • Sound, voice, haptic, and display preferences

This information is intended to remain on the user's device and is not uploaded to a custom Shape Stories backend because the app does not use a custom backend or user account system.

4. Accounts and Registration

Shape Stories does not require users or children to create an account.

The app does not require:

  • Registration
  • Login credentials
  • Google sign-in
  • Facebook sign-in
  • Cloud profiles
  • Child usernames

5. Advertising

Shape Stories may use Google AdMob to display limited advertising. Advertising is not part of the learning activity itself and is designed to appear only at appropriate natural breaks where permitted.

Child-directed advertising: Ad requests are intended to be configured for child-directed / age-restricted treatment. Personalized advertising, interest-based advertising, and remarketing are not intended to be used for child users.

The app is designed so that advertising is not required to:

  • Complete a learning activity
  • Retry an activity
  • Receive normal learning help
  • Use accessibility assistance
  • Continue normal core progression

Shape Stories does not intentionally use child-facing rewarded advertising, banner advertising during active child gameplay, native advertising, or app-open advertising in the initial release design.

If an advertisement is unavailable, the educational experience continues normally.

6. Google AdMob and Third-Party Data Processing

Google AdMob is operated by Google LLC and may process limited technical, device, network, advertising, diagnostic, security, and ad-interaction information as necessary to provide, secure, measure, and troubleshoot advertising services.

We configure advertising for a child-directed experience in accordance with the applicable Google Play Families and Google advertising requirements.

Shape Stories is designed not to intentionally transmit prohibited persistent identifiers from child users. For a child-only release, the application is intended not to request access to the Android Advertising ID where current Google Play Families requirements prohibit it.

Google's handling of information is also governed by Google's own policies.

You can learn more here:

Google Privacy Policy

Google AdMob Help Center

7. Personalized Advertising

Shape Stories is designed as a child-directed educational application.

We do not intentionally enable personalized advertising, interest-based advertising, or remarketing for child users.

Advertising configuration and SDK behavior must remain consistent with the current Google Play Families requirements before any production advertising is enabled.

8. Android Advertising Identifier

Shape Stories is intended to be released as a preschool, child-directed application.

For a solely child-targeted release, the app is designed not to request or transmit the Android Advertising ID where such use is prohibited by Google Play Families policy.

The final production application's merged Android manifest and advertising SDK configuration are reviewed before release to ensure they match the current requirements.

9. Location Information

Shape Stories does not request precise location permission.

We do not intentionally collect a child's precise GPS location.

10. Device Permissions

Shape Stories is designed to operate without requesting unnecessary sensitive device permissions.

The app does not intentionally require access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • SMS messages
  • Call logs
  • Precise location
  • Photos or videos
  • Nearby devices
  • Calendar
  • Health information

11. Analytics

Shape Stories does not use Firebase Analytics or a custom server-based analytics system in its planned initial release.

Limited progress statistics may be calculated and stored locally on the device to help parents understand what learning activities have been completed.

Google Play and Google AdMob may provide aggregated platform, stability, advertising, or performance reporting to the developer as part of their services.

12. Purchases and Payments

Shape Stories does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, Google Play Billing, or child-facing purchases in its planned initial release.

Children are not asked to enter payment information.

13. External Links and Parent Zone

Child-facing gameplay is designed not to expose ordinary external web links.

Links such as this Privacy Policy, developer information, licenses, or other external resources should be placed inside the Parent Zone and protected by an adult-oriented gate where appropriate.

14. Data Sharing

We do not sell children's personal information.

We do not operate a commercial database of child profiles.

Data may be processed by Google or other required platform infrastructure only to the extent necessary to provide services such as Google Play distribution, advertising where enabled, security, diagnostics, and app functionality.

Any third-party SDK included in the final application must be reviewed for compatibility with the app's child-directed privacy obligations before release.

15. Data Retention

Local game progress remains on the device until it is removed by the user, the app's Reset Progress function is used, the app is uninstalled, or Android clears the application's stored data.

Shape Stories does not maintain a custom cloud copy of this local progress.

Data processed independently by Google services is subject to Google's own retention practices and policies.

16. Deleting Local Progress

Parents can delete the game's locally stored learning progress using the Reset Progress option inside the Parent Zone.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its application data through Android settings may also remove local game data.

Because Shape Stories does not use user accounts or a custom cloud profile, there is no remote Shape Stories account that needs to be deleted.

17. Security

We use reasonable technical and product-design measures intended to minimize privacy risks.

These measures include limiting data collection, avoiding child accounts, avoiding unnecessary permissions, storing learning progress locally, restricting external links, and reviewing third-party SDK behavior before production release.

However, no software or electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

18. International Users

Shape Stories may be made available in multiple countries.

We aim to design the app consistently with applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play Families requirements.

Specific legal obligations may vary by country or region.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, SDKs, data practices, advertising configuration, legal requirements, or Google Play requirements change.

When we update this policy, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

We encourage parents and caregivers to review this page periodically.
